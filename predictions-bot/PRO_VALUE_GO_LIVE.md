# Pro value picks: go-live criteria, switch, checklist and weekly report

Status: SHADOW ONLY. Nothing here is switched on. `PRO_VALUE_MODE` defaults to `shadow`, and that is the
current behaviour. Live Pro and live Bet of the Day are unchanged until the owner flips the switch.

## 1. Go-live criteria

All must hold before the switch is flipped. Each weekly report (section 5) checks them.

1. **Sample size and time.** At least **40 shadow picks**, AND at least **two weeks after the 1 Nov 2026 OddsAPI
   credit reset** (i.e. on or after 15 Nov 2026). Both are required: the later of the two gates the decision.
   The two-week clock starts at the reset, not at the first pick.
2. **CLV.** Average CLV above **0%**. CLV = SportyBet price at selection / closing fair price - 1.
3. **Beat close.** More than **half** of picks beat the closing price (CLV > 0).
4. **Data quality.** No unexplained mismatches, no stale prices (sharp quote older than
   `PRO_VALUE_MAX_PRICE_AGE_MIN`), and no failed closing captures on picks that kicked off in the capture window.
5. **Bet of the Day.** Bet of the Day does not regularly lose its top candidate to Pro (see the report's
   `botd lost-to-Pro rate`; the owner sets the "regularly" threshold when reviewing).

## 2. The switch

`PRO_VALUE_MODE` (`shadow` | `publish`). Any other value is refused at start-up.

- `shadow` (default): the shadow runner and scheduled closing capture run; nothing is published; live Bet of the
  Day ignores Pro entirely.
- `publish`: Pro picks come from the Pro value selection, and live Bet of the Day excludes every event Pro has
  picked (Pro-first).

Turning the switch back to `shadow` must restore current behaviour exactly. Both directions are covered by tests
in `tests/test_pro_value_mode.py`.

**Not yet wired (must be done before go-live):** publishing Pro picks through the live Worker/D1 path, and the
hand-off from the shadow run to the generation job's Bet of the Day selection. The switch and the exclusion logic
exist and are tested; the live publish path is not connected.

## 3. "No Pro pick today" message (proposed wording, not in use)

> No Pro pick today: nothing met our value checks. Bet of the Day is unchanged.

Shown only in `publish` mode, on days when the Pro selection is empty. Not sent in shadow mode.

## 4. Go-live checklist

Before flipping to `publish`:

- [ ] Criteria in section 1 all met on the latest weekly report, with the report's numbers recorded in the change.
- [ ] Production env has `PRO_VALUE_MODE=shadow` and the change to `publish` is a single env edit, not a code change.
- [ ] `tests/test_pro_value_mode.py` passes on the release commit.
- [ ] Closing-capture job has run without failures for the previous 7 days (see report, data problems).
- [ ] Credit balance is above the 80-credit reserve by enough to cover the day's generation (9) and closing (5) calls.
- [ ] The "No Pro pick today" wording in section 3 is approved.

On day one after flipping:

- [ ] Check the generation run log: Pro picks count, BotD exclusion count, and any exceptions in the Pro path.
- [ ] Check the published Pro message and the BotD message on Telegram match the selection output.
- [ ] Check the next closing pass captured CLV for the day's picks.
- [ ] Check the daily credit spend against the expected ~14/day.

Rollback: set `PRO_VALUE_MODE=shadow` in the production env and re-run generation (or wait for the next scheduled
run). Live output returns to the pre-switch behaviour. No data is deleted; shadow rows remain in the shadow DB.

## 5. Weekly shadow report

Generated by `tools/pro_value_report.py` from the shadow DB, read-only. Fields:

- picks (selected, shadow): total and this week
- CLV: average and median; beat-close rate (share of picks with CLV > 0)
- credits used this week, from the closing-spend record, and the latest remaining balance
- botd lost-to-Pro rate: share of Bet of the Day candidates that Pro would have taken
- data problems: picks past kickoff with no closing capture; picks with stale sharp quotes; closing captures refused
- criteria: each criterion above marked met / not met

The report is a recommendation input only. It does not change any setting.

## 6. Known race between the closing job and generation (accepted, 26 Oct 2026)

The shadow DB is shared through the Actions cache. Generation (`stage5-acceptance-production`) and the 30-minute
closing job (`pro-value-closing-shadow`) each restore the latest copy, write to it and save a new one. The closing
job skips while generation is queued or running, but it only checks at its own start, so the two can still overlap.

**Worst case (accepted for now):** the closing job restores before generation saves and saves after it. The later
save then overwrites the earlier one, which loses that day's shadow rows and capture records. Nothing reaches D1 or
any user. The daily cap can be under-counted once, so that day's closing calls could reach about 10 (2x the cap of
5), still subject to the live 80-credit reserve check. No overlap occurred on 25 Oct (the first closing run was at
15:14 UTC).

**Before go-live:** a fix is required (for example a dedicated concurrency group that both workflows use, or a
per-run DB key). Go-live needs that fix or a fresh decision.
